Aerostack2 ROS 2 · open source · BSD-3

An open-source framework, built on ROS 2, for making drones fly on their own: one drone or a whole swarm, from flying by hand to complete autonomous missions. It's developed at CVAR-UPM and grew out of Aerostack, which the group has used since 2016.

Any drone, same code
The same mission runs on a Crazyflie, a DJI Tello, a Pixhawk, a DJI Matrice or in simulation; only the platform plugin changes.
Plugins all the way
State estimators, controllers and behaviors are plugins: swap one and the rest of the system doesn't notice.
Ready-made behaviors
Take off, go to, follow a path, land, trajectories, swarm flocking, started and watched from a mission.
Missions your way
Written as a Python script or as a behavior tree.

A2RL Drone Championship Abu Dhabi

A2RL, the Abu Dhabi Autonomous Racing League, holds races where nobody is at the controls. In its drone championship, run with the Drone Champions League, drones flown by AI race through a track of gates at up to 150 km/h.

The catch: every team flies the same drone, and it sees almost nothing. One forward-facing camera and an IMU, with a small computer on board; no LiDAR, no stereo, no GPS, nothing outside telling it where it is. So the race is won in the software: finding the gates in the picture, working out from them where the drone is, and flying the line as fast as it dares.

Season 1 2025

11–12 April 2025 · ADNEC Marina Hall · $1M prize · 14 teams

Billed as the first race for AI drones only. MAVLab from TU Delft won the AI Grand Challenge, two laps in 17 seconds, and its drone beat top human pilots head to head.

CVAR-UPM: one of the top eight finalists, and second in our multi-drone race, flying both laps on its own with only the camera and the IMU.

Season 2 2026

21–22 January 2026 · UMEX, ADNEC · $600,000 prize

TII Racing set the fastest lap, 12.032 s, ahead of MAVLab's 12.832 s; MAVLab won the Gold multi-drone race. Human vs AI went to the last of nine races at 4–4, and world champion Minchan Kim took it when the AI drone clipped a gate.

CVAR-UPM: second in the Silver multi-drone race and the sixth-fastest lap of the championship, three times faster than the year before.

Our team: CVAR-UPM

We race with Aerostack2, the same framework pushed to racing speed. For 2026 the drone got a new MPCC controller and positioning from several gates at once. I work on state estimation: knowing where the drone is and how it's moving, with nothing but the camera and the IMU to go on.

  1. Camera and IMUAll the drone has to go on.
  2. Gate detectionFinds the gates in the picture.
  3. My partState estimationWhere the drone is and how it's moving, from the gates it sees (PnP, several at once) and the IMU.
  4. ControlMPCC flies the racing line.
