HeartGold on One Screen

Pokémon HeartGold running natively on Windows and Android, made playable on a single touchscreen, the way the Game Boy Advance games are played: every screen the DS splits over its two screens is drawn again for one. This page follows that work, screen by screen.

- of - screens done

How it works

One picture at a time

The window shows one of the DS's two screens: the map and the battles from the top one, the menus and apps from the bottom one. The screen you look at is always the one you can touch.

Menus as buttons

The start menu, every YES/NO, the scripts' choices and the battle's commands and moves become buttons drawn on the top screen in the look of HeartGold's bottom-screen buttons, for touch, D-pad or mouse.

The top screen comes along

What the other screen held is brought over: an item's description, a held item, whose name you're typing, the clock, a place's name. Pages the DS showed side by side become pages you turn.

The other screen, any time

Holding Space shows the other DS screen, for whatever isn't drawn again yet.
